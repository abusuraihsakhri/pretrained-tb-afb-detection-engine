# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Project participation must be respectful, evidence-based, and safe for people
whose data or care may be affected by biomedical software. Contributors should:

- Critique methods and evidence without personal attacks.
- Protect patient privacy and confidential source data.
- State uncertainty and correct errors transparently.
- Avoid fabricated results, citations, credentials, or clinical claims.
- Respect source licenses, authorship, and contributor attribution.
- Welcome questions from contributors with different technical backgrounds.

Harassment, discriminatory conduct, doxxing, threats, deliberate privacy
violations, and knowingly deceptive scientific reporting are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the repository owner’s
GitHub contact channel. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict
participation when necessary to protect contributors, research participants, or
the integrity of the project.

This policy applies in repository discussions, pull requests, reviews, and
other spaces where a participant represents the project.
