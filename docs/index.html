Correction pathway

From raw provenance to a locked test

  1. Verify sources.Record pinned version, license, classes, annotation type, acquisition metadata, and exclusions.
  2. Correct annotations.Exclude quarantined records and reject malformed or out-of-bounds boxes without silent repair.
  3. Group before splitting.Keep each patient, specimen, smear/slide, original field, and duplicate cluster in one split.
  4. Develop without the test set.Use training and development validation for model and threshold selection.
  5. Evaluate once.Run the locked test with frozen hashes, confidence intervals, source-level results, and error analysis.

Limits and scope

What this system does not establish

  • AFB appearance does not identify Mycobacterium tuberculosis or confirm tuberculosis.
  • No prospective, external-site, reader, workflow, calibration, or clinical-impact validation has been completed.
  • Performance across laboratories, scanners, stain protocols, populations, and low-load specimens remains unknown.
  • Smear grading requires a validated acquisition and field-sampling protocol; detections alone are insufficient.
  • The local research log is not a regulated electronic-record audit trail.
