TRANSLATIONAL PATHOLOGY

Clinical use cases & diagnostic deployment scenarios.

Engineered to integrate seamlessly into routine mycobacteriology laboratories, high-volume screening hubs, and decentralized clinics:

USE CASE 01

Automated Routine Smear Triage

Rapidly triages high-volume sputum smear slides in high-burden screening centers. Automatically filters out high-confidence negative fields to relieve laboratory backlogs and focus pathologist attention on scanty and low-density positives.

USE CASE 02

Whole Slide Image (WSI) Mapping

Integrates with digital slide scanners (Aperio, Hamamatsu, 3DHISTECH) via OpenSlide. Performs 640×640 boundary-aware sliding-window scanning with adaptive overlap to map AFB clusters across entire gigapixel glass surfaces.

USE CASE 03

Decentralized & Edge Telepathology

Lightweight 3.01M parameter architecture delivers ultra-fast 2.5 ms inference per tile. Enables real-time on-device screening on portable laboratory microscopes and mobile diagnostic units without requiring cloud connectivity.

USE CASE 04

Standardized IUATLD / WHO Smear Grading

Translates microscopic rod counts into standardized international IUATLD/WHO semi-quantitative smear grades (Negative, Scanty, 1+, 2+, 3+), eliminating inter-observer variability and standardizing clinical reporting.

CLINICAL QUANTITATION

IUATLD / WHO Smear Grading Protocol.

The engine automatically quantifies identified AFB bacilli and normalizes bacillary density across examined fields using international IUATLD/WHO standards:

IUATLD / WHO Grade Microscopic Examination Criteria (1000× Oil Immersion HPF) Clinical Interpretation & Infectivity
Negative 0 AFB observed across 100 High-Power Fields (HPF) No acid-fast bacilli detected
Scanty 1 – 9 AFB observed across 100 High-Power Fields Low count; exact number reported (e.g. Scanty 4/100)
1+ 10 – 99 AFB observed across 100 High-Power Fields Positive bacillary load
2+ 1 – 10 AFB per single HPF (evaluated in 50 fields) Moderate-positive load
3+ > 10 AFB per single HPF (evaluated in 20 fields) High-positive load (highly infectious)

Clinical intelligence built for digital pathology.

Automated OpenSlide tiling processes gigapixel slides (.svs, .ndpi, .tiff) with intelligent background skipping to ignore empty glass.
